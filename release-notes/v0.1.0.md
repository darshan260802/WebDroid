# WebDroid 0.1.0 — first release

Turn any website into an app that lives inside WebDroid.

## Highlights
- **Web apps without browser chrome**: each app gets its own card in Recents, a branded splash, and system bars in the site's colour.
- **uBlock Origin built in**: uBO's real engine and default lists, with network blocking, cosmetic filters and scriptlets. Lists update in the background.
- **Native video player**: takes over page videos (Auto, Button or Off per app). Gestures for brightness, volume and seek; double-tap ±10 s; pinch to zoom; hold for 2×. Audio, subtitle and quality tracks, speed, screen lock, picture-in-picture, background play, resume and download.
- **Pull to refresh, pull further for the menu**, with an optional mini toolbar.
- **Per-app options**: separate login, desktop site, force dark, fingerprint lock, open other sites in the browser.
- **Home-screen shortcuts** that open the app inside WebDroid.
- Clay-inspired design: cream canvas, saturated cards, springy animations.

## Install
Download `WebDroid-v0.1.0.apk` below and open it on your phone. You may need to allow installs from your browser or file manager.
