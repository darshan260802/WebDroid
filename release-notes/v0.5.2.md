# WebDroid 0.5.2: real Liquid Glass

- Glass now behaves like a lens instead of frosted plastic: much less blur, content stays visible through it, and its rounded rim bends what's beneath it (with a hint of colour fringing at the edges).
- Light catches the rim as a thin highlight and glows softly inside the lit edge; the far edge falls into shade, so glass looks thick.
- Round glass buttons look like drops of glass.
- Slide your finger across the tab bar: the selection lifts like a lens and magnifies the tab beneath it.
- Menus and alerts stay a little frostier so their text remains easy to read. Android 12 keeps frosted glass; Reduce Transparency still turns glass off.
