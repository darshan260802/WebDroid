# WebDroid 0.2.0: notifications and seamless sign-in

## Notifications for web apps
Android's WebView has no web notifications, so WebDroid adds them:
- Sites can ask for notification permission with a native prompt. Their `new Notification()` and `registration.showNotification()` calls become real Android notifications.
- Each app has its own notification channel, so you can pick its sound and style in Android settings. Notifications group per app and keep the site's icon.
- Tapping a notification brings the app back, and the page reacts as it would in a browser (for example, it opens that chat).
- **Stay connected in background** (per-app option): keeps chat or mail apps running so their notifications arrive while you're elsewhere. A quiet status notification shows while it's on.
- Settings has a shortcut to allow unrestricted background activity for reliability.

## Sign-in, cookies and storage
- WebDroid no longer marks its requests as coming from an embedded WebView, which fixes "This browser may not be secure" on Google and similar sign-ins.
- Login cookies are saved to disk as soon as a page finishes loading.
- Native username/password dialog for sites with HTTP authentication.
- Apps reopen on the page you left, for up to 6 hours.

## Also new
- `navigator.share()` opens the Android share sheet; `window.print()` opens Android printing.
- Quick-start suggestions on an empty home screen.
