# WebDroid 0.5.0: a calm new design

WebDroid has been redesigned from top to bottom, following Apple's design guidelines and its approach to fluid motion.

## Look and feel
- Calm system colours in light and dark, and your phone's own font.
- Liquid Glass for the bars, buttons and menus that float over your content. It bends and blurs what's beneath it on Android 13 and later, is frosted on Android 12, and is solid on older phones.
- Settings → Appearance → **Reduce Transparency** swaps glass for solid surfaces.
- Motion follows your finger: sheets, menus and screens move on springs, keep the speed of your swipe, and can be caught mid-animation. Removing animations in Android's accessibility settings makes them simple fades.

## Finding your way
- A glass tab bar: **Apps**, **Privacy** and **Settings**. Slide across it to switch tabs.
- Large titles shrink into the top bar as you scroll.
- The Back gesture shows the previous screen as you swipe, and you can change your mind halfway.

## Apps
- Your web apps sit on a home-screen grid.
- Touch and hold an app for its menu (Open, Edit, Add to Home Screen, Delete). Keep holding and drag to rearrange.
- Adding or editing an app opens a sheet: name and icon fill in from the website, and you can pick a colour, video behaviour, privacy options and a VPN. With unsaved changes, WebDroid asks before closing it.

## Privacy
- The ad blocker, filter lists, Tor and your WireGuard and proxy VPNs now have their own tab.

## Inside apps
- The pull-down menu, app settings, link menu, permission requests and sign-in prompts are redesigned. Clearing a site's data now asks first.
- The video player has clear-glass controls and a white progress bar.
