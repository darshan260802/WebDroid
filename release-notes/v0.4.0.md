# WebDroid 0.4.0: per-site VPN and a better video player

## Per-site VPN (free & open source)
- Choose a VPN for each web app: **Tor** (built in, no account), a **WireGuard** config (e.g. Proton VPN's free plan or your own server), or a **SOCKS5/HTTP proxy** (Orbot, Shadowsocks clients, your own).
- Only that app's sites go through it, including the CDNs and APIs they load; other apps keep connecting directly.
- If the VPN is down, the app's sites don't load, so nothing falls back to your real connection.
- Tor: pick an exit country. The first connection takes a little while; its progress shows on screen.
- WebRTC can't reveal your IP in VPN apps. The video player and downloads use the app's VPN too.
- Settings → VPN: import or paste WireGuard configs, add proxies, and check the exit IP and country.

## Video player
- Catches more videos: ones you tap to play (also after the page navigates), videos inside embedded players, and streams whose addresses don't look like video. Going fullscreen on a site offers the WebDroid player.
- Fewer black screens: streams are sent with the site's cookies and referrer, and unknown stream types are detected. If a stream still can't play, it goes back to the page instead of a black screen.
- Seek bar: press anywhere on it to jump, drag the thumb to scrub, with a time bubble.
- Long videos scrub further per swipe.
- In a site's own fullscreen player, its seek bar and buttons work normally again.
